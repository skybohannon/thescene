# The Scene (2004–2006) — Frames

Nineteen frames from the two seasons: the windows the story turns on, reproduced at the size they were captured. They are the same frames the synopses use, gathered in one place, each with its caption and a link back to its episode.

The captions say what each frame means, and that gives the story away. The series and its frames remain Jun Group's; they are reproduced here alongside commentary on what each one shows.
