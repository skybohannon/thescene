A spoiler-free companion for watching The Scene and The Scene 2.0 for the first time: one line per episode, saying where it starts and nothing about where it goes. When you've watched an episode, its full entry is one click away. Those entries give everything away, including what later episodes reveal, so it's worth waiting.

Nearly all of the series is text on a screen. IRC channels, instant messages, mail and FTP windows do the work that dialogue does elsewhere, and several of the moments that matter are on screen for only a few seconds. Watch somewhere you can pause, and read everything. The episodes are free on the Internet Archive: season 1 and The Scene 2.0.
