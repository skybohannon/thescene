The Scene (2004–2006)

The Scene is a web series in two twenty-episode seasons about warez groups and the people in them, told almost entirely through on-screen text: IRC channels, instant messages, mail and FTP windows that the viewer reads rather than hears. The databases that list it leave the plot fields blank. These are the missing synopses, reconstructed from every episode's screen text and audio.

They are written for someone who has watched the series, or doesn't mind knowing how it ends.

Haven't watched it yet? Start with the spoiler-free episode guide: one line per episode, nothing about how it ends.

  • Season 1 · 2004–2006 · 20 episodes The Scene A release group, a screener sold on the side, and an informant in the channel from before the first scene.
  • Season 2 · 2006 · 20 episodes The Scene 2.0 An ex-encoder recruited back, a daughter held as leverage, and two handlers telling her incompatible things in the same minute.
  • Both seasons Characters and Handles Who every handle belongs to, who plays them, and where to find them.

Read it offline

Each edition also comes as a single file with its frames built in, which works without a connection, including next to the episodes themselves: season 1, The Scene 2.0, the character index and the episode guide. Save the page to keep it.

Watch it

Both seasons are free on the Internet Archive: season 1 and The Scene 2.0. Background, cast and credits are on Wikipedia and IMDb.

How it was made

Every episode was sampled every three seconds and read with OCR, and the spoken parts were transcribed with Whisper. The pipeline, its known limits, and why the recovered text itself isn't published are in the repository.

If you watch an episode and something here doesn’t match, report a correction — a timestamp is the most useful thing you can give.
